1. Canva & Google Slides

Create Attractive Lesson Slides

How to Use - Step by Step:

1

Open canva.com → Search "Education Presentation" → Choose free template

2

Add your lesson text, drag photos, change to Marathi/Hindi font, add icons

3

Download as PDF/MP4 and share on WhatsApp or show on projector

✅ Saves 2 hours daily
✅ 100% Mobile Friendly

2. YouTube & DIKSHA

Free Curriculum Videos for Students

How to Use:

1

Go to diksha.gov.in → Select Class (e.g., Class 5) → Select Subject → Select Chapter

2

Search on YouTube: "Solar System Class 5 Marathi" → Preview and save playlist

3

Connect laptop to projector → Play video → Students understand 3x faster with visuals

3. Kahoot & Google Forms

Fun Quizzes & Instant Assessment

How to Use:

👉 Kahoot: kahoot.com → Create → Add 5 MCQs → Click Play → Share Game PIN → Students join on kahoot.it with phone → Live leaderboard!
👉 Google Forms: forms.google.com → Blank form → Add questions → Enable "Quiz mode" for auto marks → Share link on WhatsApp → Results in Google Sheet automatically!

4. Google Classroom & WhatsApp

Share Homework in 2 Minutes

1️⃣ Google Classroom: classroom.google.com → Create Class → Share class code → Post Assignment with video/link → Students submit → You give marks
2️⃣ WhatsApp Broadcast: Create Group "Class 5A Parents" → Daily post: 1 Video + 1 Homework PDF + 1 Quiz Link → Parents stay updated
💡 Real Impact: One teacher can share homework to 40 students in 2 minutes instead of writing on board for 15 minutes!
