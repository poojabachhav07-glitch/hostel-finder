TOOL 1: Canva Slides

Make beautiful lesson in 5 minutes, not 2 hours

MOST USED

HOW TO USE - 3 EASY STEPS

1
Go to Canva.com & Search

Type "Education Presentation" → 1000+ free templates. Select one with colors. No need to design from scratch.

2
Add Your Content

Replace text with your lesson (e.g., Water Cycle). Drag your school photos. Change font to Noto Sans Devanagari for Marathi. Add animation - Fade In.

3
Download & Show

Click Share → Download → PDF. Send on WhatsApp group or connect to projector via HDMI. Students love animations!

Teacher from SNJ School made "Solar System" slides in 6 minutes using this. Students said "Wow!"

TOOL 2: DIKSHA & YouTube

Free government videos - Offline also works

GOVT FREE

▶️ VIDEO 1: HOW TO USE DIKSHA APP

▶️ VIDEO 2: FIND BEST EDUCATIONAL VIDEOS

HOW TO USE - 3 EASY STEPS

1
Open diksha.gov.in or DIKSHA App

Select: Maharashtra → Marathi → Class 5 → Science. All chapters will show with QR codes.

2
Scan Textbook QR Code

Every Balbharati textbook page has QR code. Scan with DIKSHA app → Video of exact lesson opens instantly. No searching!

3
Download & Play Offline

Click Download button. Video saves offline. In school with no internet? Still plays on projector. 100% free.

TOOL 3: Kahoot! Game Quiz

Students shout answers - Full energy class!

STUDENTS LOVE

HOW TO USE - LIVE DEMO INCLUDED

Step 1: Go to kahoot.com → Sign Up Free (Teacher) → Create → Add question + 4 options + correct answer + image
Step 2: Click Play → Classic → Screen shows Game PIN (e.g., 123456). Students go to kahoot.it on phone and enter PIN + name.
Step 3: You show question on projector, students answer on phone in 20 sec. Live leaderboard shows! Winner gets prize. Revision = Game!
